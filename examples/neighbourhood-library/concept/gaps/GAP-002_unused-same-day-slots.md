## Description
When a same-day room is still free at a given hour, should it become
bookable in advance for the following days, or stay reserved?

## Context and impact
Opened by DEC-002: splitting the rooms creates slots that may stay
empty on quiet days, which the library management will notice.

## Review trigger
Two months of data on same-day occupancy after DEC-002 goes live.
