## Hypothesis
If two of the four rooms can be booked only on the same day, fewer
walk-ins are turned away, and no-shows do not increase.

## Rationale
INS-001: most demand is same-day. Advance bookings are where no-shows
come from; same-day bookings are made by people already in the building.

## Verification method
Two-week pilot with paper slots at the front desk, compared with the
previous fortnight.

## Collected evidence
- 2026-04-10 — turn-aways down from 61 to 22; no-shows stable (9 → 8).
  Synthetic data for this example.
