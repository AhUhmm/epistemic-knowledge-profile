## Description
A screen at the entrance showing which rooms are free now and in the
next hour, so that walk-ins know before queueing at the desk.

## Why it matters
It would shorten the queue at peak hours, when the desk is busiest.

## Activation conditions
Depends on DEC-002 being in place (same-day slots must exist to be
shown) and on a budget line for the hardware, not yet discussed.
